1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------
MedTrust AI investigates whether predictive confidence alone is sufficient for deciding whether a clinical prediction should be accepted.

The framework combines predictive confidence with an Explanation Reliability Score (ERS) to produce an ACCEPT, REVIEW, or DEFER routing decision.

2. DATASET AND TASK
------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset: MIMIC-IV Clinical Database DEMO v2.2
Task: ICU mortality prediction
Outcome: Death during the same ICU stay
Index time: ICU admission
Feature window: First 6 hours after ICU admission
Split: Patient-level train / validation / test split
Test cases: 23

3. MODEL PIPELINE

Explanation method: SHAP
Reliability signals: stability, faithfulness, ranking consistency, and sign consistency

7. INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------
The demo experiment demonstrates the routing mechanism by combining predictive confidence and explanation reliability.

The comparison shows how adding ERS can change routing decisions for cases where predictive confidence is high but explanation reliability is comparatively low.

These results are intended as a methodological demonstration on the MIMIC-IV DEMO dataset, not as clinical performance evidence.

8. IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
1. The MIMIC-IV DEMO dataset is very small.
2. The number of ICU mortality events is very small.
3. ERS components are prototype reliability measures and require stronger validation.
4. Calibration estimates are unstable on a tiny dataset.
5. The current experiment does not establish clinical utility or safety.
6. Final research experiments should use the full MIMIC-IV dataset.
7. External validation using eICU-CRD remains required.
8. Temporal and out-of-distribution evaluation remains required.

9. NEXT RESEARCH PHASE
------------------------------------------------------------
